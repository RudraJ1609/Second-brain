PARA Method Cheatsheet
======================

Projects — short-term efforts with a clear outcome (e.g. "Ship SecondSelf Week 1").
Areas — ongoing responsibilities with no end date (e.g. health, career, finances).
Resources — topics of interest and reference material (e.g. RAG, embeddings).
Archives — inactive items from the other three categories.

Capture rule: if you are unsure, put it in Resources and refile later.
Linking rule: prefer fewer high-confidence links over many weak ones.
